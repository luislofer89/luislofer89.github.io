I’m Luis López Fernández, an AI Engineer and Project Lead focused on turning complex data into real-world products. Over the last decade I’ve worked across academic, research and industry,, leading teams that build production-ready CV and AI systems.

My path into AI started in geospatial technologies: surveying, photogrammetry and 3D reconstruction. During my PhD I worked at the intersection of CV, AI and energy efficiency, using multimodal data (LiDAR, thermal and RGB imagery) to understand how buildings and infrastructure behave in the real world. That research mindset stayed with me as I moved into industry, where I’ve helped design and implement ADAS systems, large-scale spatial analytics pipelines and multiple generative AI solutions.

Today, I lead AI and innovation projects, from real-time perception solutions to spatial intelligence platforms that bring cutting-edge AI technologies into Hexagon’s portfolio. I enjoy working at the intersection of disciplines, connecting researchers, engineers and business stakeholders, and guiding teams from early experimentation to deployed systems with measurable impact.

I care deeply about responsible and human-centred AI, continuous learning and knowledge sharing through publications, talks and mentoring. If you’re interested in collaborating on AI, geospatial technologies or project leadership, I’m always open to a conversation.

Outside of work you’ll most likely find me in the mountains —mountain biking, hiking or enjoying the snow—. Sport has always been an important part of my life: I rowed and competed at national level in my youth, and today I channel that discipline and team spirit both into my prersonal and professional life. I enjoy exploring my own boundaries trough endurance sports, having participated in multipel long-distance races and currently preparing for an Ironman.
